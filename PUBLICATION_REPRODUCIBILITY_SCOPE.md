# Publication reproducibility scope

| Capability | Inputs and interpretation |
|---|---|
| Synthetic rule tests | The 17 fixed tests check event clocks, support rules, NA and text identifiers on invented cases. |
| Synthetic demonstration | Three invented histories generate two CSVs marked `SYNTHETIC_NOT_STUDY_DATA`. This workflow exercises the rules and does not validate historical study events. |
| Frozen summary displays | With all 17 authorized frozen research CSVs and their matching manifest, dictionary and contract, the runner regenerates ten display CSVs and three standalone PNG/SVG figure pairs. These displays are selected aggregate outputs and have their own layouts. The software release does not supply the research CSVs. |
| Historical study workflow | Whole-domain 30 m event production, PREP3/RISK, raw imagery, KM, matching, model fitting, bootstrap, predictive training or evaluation, independent reference validation and full manuscript figure production are outside the executable scope. |

The real display workflow retains the original and strict populations, separate S5/S10 windows, episode and distinct-land denominators, and D-star/K0 spatial support. NA remains unavailable. The original Table 2 input wording is preserved, while J6R and J7 explicitly include current and prior calendar exposure. The study's GLC v2 public archive binding is not established in the released package.
